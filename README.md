# Hospitrack
**Hospitrack** is a comprehensive, web-based Hospital Management System designed to streamline hospital operations, improve patient care coordination, and simplify healthcare administration through a centralized digital platform.

The platform provides dedicated portals for Super Administrators, Hospital Administrators, Doctors, and Patients, with role-based access control and hospital-level data isolation.

It integrates essential hospital workflows, including patient registration and admission, doctor management, consultations, prescriptions, appointments, bed management, referrals, and clinical record management.

Built using **Java 17, Spring Boot, Spring Security, PostgreSQL, HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript**, Hospitrack follows a modular architecture with RESTful APIs, JWT-based authentication, and Docker-based deployment support.

## Key Features

- Multi-role authentication and authorization
- Hospital and user management
- Patient registration and admission management
- Doctor workstation and consultation management
- Digital prescriptions and medication safety checks
- Appointment and referral management
- Hospital bed and capacity management
- Clinical records and patient health journey
- Multi-hospital data isolation
- Administrative dashboards and audit trails
- Docker-based backend deployment

## Project Goal

Create a centralized, secure, and accessible digital healthcare management platform that reduces administrative complexity and supports more efficient hospital operations.
