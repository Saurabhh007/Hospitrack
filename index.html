🔒 Institutional Credential Governance

Authorized credential issuance and security recovery protocols

Under Hospitrack's strict healthcare credential governance policy, public self-registration and self-service password resets are restricted to protect sensitive patient records and hospital operational data.
👤 For Patients

Patient portal credentials and patient IDs (PAT-xxx) are issued upon hospital intake. If you need credential recovery or password reset, contact your admitting hospital's patient registration desk.

🩺 For Medical Staff & Doctors

Clinical physician and medical staff accounts are provisioned exclusively by your Hospital Administrator. Contact your facility's IT administration or Hospital Operations desk for credential reissuance.

🏛️ For Hospital Administrators

Facility administrator accounts are onboarded and managed by the Super Administrator. To request credential updates or facility security resets, contact the Hospitrack Network Governance team.
